Money rules

Six numbers worth remembering.

50/30/20

The budget split

50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and debt. A simple starting point for any paycheck.

3–6

Months of backup

An emergency fund of 3 to 6 months of costs keeps a surprise bill off your credit card.

72

The doubling rule

72 ÷ your rate = years to double. At 8% that's 9 years. At a 24% card rate, your debt doubles in 3.

4%

The retirement rule

Withdraw about 4% a year. So $1,000,000 pays roughly $40,000 a year, before taxes.

30%

Credit use

Keep card balances under 30% of your limits. Lower is better for your credit score.

100%

Free match

If your job matches 401(k) money, put in enough to get all of it. It's an instant 100% return.
